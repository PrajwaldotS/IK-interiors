The Client's Challenge

Mr. Mohammed had recently bought an apartment in Attapur. The kitchen footprint was a compact 11 ft x 9 ft with heavy daily usage, high spice cooking, and continuous steam exposure:

  • Staining & Yellowing Concerns: Frequent use of turmeric and tadka spices easily leaves permanent yellow marks on cheap laminates.
  • Boiling Water Exposure: Deep sink washing and hot utensils put traditional BWR plywood at risk of delamination.
  • Dead Corner Storage: An existing civil concrete counter blocked access to a 3-foot blind corner where utensils were wasted.

The IK Solution & Engineering

IK Interiors completely re-engineered the kitchen workflow with certified BWP 710 marine plywood and premium Italian anti-fingerprint acrylics:

Century Club Prime BWP 710 Construction

Manufactured with boiling-water-proof phenolic resins, zero surface voids, and glue-line anti-termite protection. Fully rated for 100% water & fire resistance.

2mm Anti-Yellowing Acrylic Shutters

Treated with UV-stable and chemical-resistant top layers that wipe clean of turmeric, chili oil, and grease with a simple microfiber damp wipe without residue.

Ebco Bi-Fold Lift-Up Systems

Installed smooth hydraulic bi-fold overhead cabinets, giving seamless two-tier access without hitting head space during busy cooking hours.
