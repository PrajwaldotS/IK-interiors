Duplex Villa False Ceiling & Track Ambiance

How IK Interiors solved extreme acoustic echo and ambient lighting challenges in a 21-foot double-height villa living room using Saint-Gobain Gyproc fireline boards, acoustic rafters, and 48V magnetic tracks.

The Client's Challenge

Mr. Sharma’s custom duplex villa in Bandlaguda Jagir featured an impressive 21-foot double-height atrium living hall. While architecturally grand, it presented acute everyday livability problems:

  • Echo & Reverberation: Hard stone flooring and 21-foot concrete slab created a cavernous hollow echo, making normal family conversation tiring.
  • Harsh Single Chandelier Lighting: A heavy single chandelier caused harsh glare downward while leaving upper gallery walkways dark and uninviting.
  • Roof Slab Heat Dissipation: The uninsulated top concrete slab radiated extreme summer heat into the second-floor gallery.

The IK Solution & Engineering

Kamalesh Swamy implemented a multi-dimensional ceiling architecture combining acoustic damping, magnetic track flexibility, and certified fire resistance:

Saint-Gobain Gyproc Fireline & Thermal Barrier

Constructed on heavy-gauge G.I. framing with certified Saint-Gobain Gyproc 12.5mm Fireline moisture-resistant boards. A rockwool insulation backing blocks radiant roof heat, reducing room temperature by 4°C.

48V Low-Voltage Magnetic Track System

Embedded flush magnetic tracks allowing the homeowner to effortlessly click, slide, and rotate spotlight heads, linear diffusers, and pendant drops to spotlight artwork and reading areas without rewiring.

Acoustic Fluted Timber Rafters

Parallel acoustic wooden louvers installed across the double-height transition zone break sound waves, completely resolving room echo and lending warm architectural grandeur.
