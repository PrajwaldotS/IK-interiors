The Client's Challenge

The client, a senior physician at a private hospital in Gachibowli, wanted a master bedroom that mirrored the look of a high-end luxury boutique hotel suite. The challenge lay in:

  • High-Ceiling Scale (10.5 ft): Standard 7-foot wardrobes left ugly gaps at the top, collecting dust and breaking the aesthetic grandeur.
  • Termite & Moisture Concerns: Ground seepage in the villa's east wall meant conventional MDF wardrobes would deteriorate within years.
  • Dark Interior Syndrome: Deep wardrobes often make clothes hard to see without adequate interior lighting.

The IK Solution & Engineering

IK Interiors planned a complete 10.5-foot floor-to-ceiling sliding wardrobe system with integrated dressing mirror and automated ambient sensors:

Bronze Tinted Toughened Glass

Framed with slim-line matte gold anodized aluminum profiles, the tinted glass panels offer partial translucency when backlights are activated and reflect like sleek mirrors when off.

Automated Motion Sensor Lighting

Concealed warm 3000K LED aluminum profile channels illuminate each bay automatically when a slider door is glided open, turning wardrobe browsing into an experience.

100% Water & Fire Resistant Carcass

Constructed with Century Club Prime BWP plywood and anti-fungal backboards with zero termite risk, backed by our ironclad 15-year warranty.
