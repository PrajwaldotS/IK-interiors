The Client's Challenge

The client has an extensive collection of ethnic designer wear, fine watches, and jewelry. Her duplex villa in Rajendranagar had a large dressing room (16 ft x 10 ft) with substandard storage:

  • No Central Jewelry Organisation: Watches, sunglasses, and precious necklaces were scattered inside random boxes with poor visibility.
  • Dust Accumulation on Sarees: Open shelving allowed dust to settle on expensive silk embroidery and evening wear.
  • Adjacent En-Suite Moisture: The master bathroom shared a common wall, threatening traditional MDF boards with dampness and wood rot.

The IK Solution & Engineering

Kamalesh Swamy orchestrated a high-fashion private boutique dressing layout with a standalone central island dresser and sealed fluted glass enclosures:

Central Island Dresser with Glass Showcase

A dual-sided island dresser topped with 10mm toughened clear glass, revealing custom Hettich velvet-lined pullout organizer trays for watches, jewelry, and belts with internal edge lighting.

100% Water & Fire Resistant Substrate

Built with Century BWP 710 marine plywood and moisture-resistant backing along the bathroom partition, completely mitigating dampness with zero fungal degradation.

Fluted Glass & Automatic Sensor Illumination

Acoustic brush-sealed aluminium fluted glass doors prevent dust ingress while concealed 3000K LED hanging rail sensors illuminate each section automatically on door opening.
