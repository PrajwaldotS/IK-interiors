The Client's Challenge

The homeowners had moved into their newly acquired independent villa in Sun City, Bandlaguda Jagir. The builder-grade kitchen provided had several critical issues:

  • High Moisture & Dampness: The sink zone had visible dampness creeping from the plumbing shaft, putting standard particle boards at risk of rotting.
  • Inadequate Cooking Space: Traditional two-counter setup restricted family cooking and left dead corners with zero usability.
  • Fire & Heat Vulnerability: The stove vicinity lacked certified fire-retardant backing, causing safety concerns with high-heat Indian cooking.

The IK Solution & Engineering

Founder Kamalesh Swamy conducted on-site laser measurements and prepared a personalized 3D (If Required) layout that completely reimagined the kitchen into an open-plan island layout:

100% Water & Fire Resistant Substrate

All sink-base units and tall pantries were built using 100% waterproof UPVC composite modules and Greenply 710 BWP Marine Grade plywood that never swell under leakage and carry certified Class-1 fire resistance.

Quartz Island Breakfast Counter

A 7-foot standalone island with 20mm seamless white quartz countertop serves as both a high-capacity prep table and an everyday dining bar with under-counter cutlery drawers.

German Soft-Close Motion

Fully fitted with genuine Hettich InnoTech Atira drawer channels capable of bearing 40kg each, Häfele soft-close 110° clip hinges, and an Ebco pull-out spice rack.
