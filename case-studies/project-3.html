The Client's Challenge

Mr. Srinivas lives in an independent ground-floor villa in Sai Balaji Nagar, Bandlaguda Jagir. Due to soil moisture conditions in the zone, his previous wooden kitchen and bathroom vanities faced severe challenges:

  • Devastating Termite Infestation: Despite chemical pest treatments, subterranean termites hollowed out previous sink baseboards within 2 years.
  • Constant Sink Seepage: Pipe leaks and heavy washing in the utility wet kitchen resulted in swollen wood, mold smells, and peeling laminates.
  • Fire Safety Hesitation: Cooking zones near the heavy cylinder pipeline required high fire tolerance, which traditional particle board cabinets could not assure.

The IK Solution & Engineering

IK Interiors founder Kamalesh Swamy recommended an absolute zero-wood, 100% UPVC carcass architecture that is completely inert to moisture and pest organisms:

100% Virgin UPVC Hollow-Core Extrusions

The entirety of the carcass, shelving, and sink basins were fabricated from heavy-density extruded UPVC polymers that do not contain cellulose, making it scientifically impossible for termites or borer beetles to feed or breed.

Complete Submersion Water Resistance

Even during water pipe bursting or deep floor washing, the UPVC framework absorbs 0.0% water. Shutters are finished with waterproof polymer membranes with laser seamless edging.

Self-Extinguishing Fire Retardancy

UPVC features natural chlorine-based self-extinguishing chemistry that satisfies strict fire safety standards, producing zero burning droplets during extreme thermal exposure.
