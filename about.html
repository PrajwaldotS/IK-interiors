Our Origin & Passion

Crafting Spaces Where Living Feels Effortless

IK Interiors Solutions is a leading interior design and manufacturing firm based in Bandlaguda Jagir, Hyderabad, Telangana. Guided by founder Kamalesh Swamy, we have spent over 12 years perfecting the balance between visual elegance, functional utility, and realistic budgets.

We believe that a successful interior begins with deep understanding of the people who live and work in the space. Rather than forcing generic templates, we listen to your family's routines, your storage requirements, and your aesthetic preferences. Through advanced engineering in 100% water and fire-resistant UPVC systems, termite-proof joinery, and precision modular cabinetry, we build interiors engineered to last decades.

Our Systematic Approach

How we ensure every residential and commercial project is delivered on schedule, on budget, and without headaches.

01

Understand Space & Intent

We listen to your lifestyle, study room layouts, assess natural lighting, and understand how your family intends to utilize every corner.

02

Preferences & Budget

We discuss material finishes (UPVC vs Marine Ply, Acrylic vs PU), color themes, functional hardware options, and align with your target budget.

03

Design & 3D Proposal

We generate realistic 3D design visuals, precise measurement drawings, and an itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with transparent pricing.

04

Precision Execution

Factory-level fabrication followed by disciplined on-site installation overseen by dedicated project supervisors with zero compromise.

05

Review & Handover

A comprehensive joint walkthrough with the customer, thorough quality checks, handover of warranty documents, and post-installation support.
