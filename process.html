Step-by-Step Delivery

A Clear Process. Spaces Designed Around Your Needs.

We take the anxiety out of home and commercial interior fitouts through structured milestones, factory pre-fabrication, and clear communication.

01

1. Initial Consultation

We meet at your property or our Bandlaguda Jagir office to understand your space, functional needs, preferred aesthetic (minimalist, modern, traditional, or industrial), target timeline, and budget parameters.

Free Site Walkthrough Lifestyle Assessment Budget Planning
02

2. Requirement Assessment & Laser Measurements

Our technical engineers conduct millimeter-precise laser measurements of walls, plumbing conduits, electrical points, ceiling levels, and moisture/termite vulnerability factors.

Laser Measurements Dampness & Termite Audit Conduit Mapping
03

3. 3D Design & Commercial Proposal (If Required)

We present comprehensive 3D photorealistic renderings, material samples (acrylics, UPVC sections, laminates), and a transparent, itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with fixed prices.

Photorealistic 3D Renders Physical Swatches Fixed Price Contract
04

4. Factory Precision & On-Site Execution

Woodworking and UPVC cutting take place using automated factory machinery to prevent dusty mess in your apartment. On-site installation is supervised directly by Kamalesh Swamy and project managers.

Factory Edge-Banded Panels Weekly Progress Updates Dust-Controlled Installation
05

5. Review, Handover & 15-Year Warranty

A 150-point checklist inspection alongside the client. We clean up, test every hinge and drawer, hand over keys, and issue your official 15-year warranty document.

Deep Site Cleaning 15-Year Warranty Certificate Post-Handover Care
