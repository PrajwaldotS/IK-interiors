1. UPVC, PVC & Plywood Interiors

Hyderabad's climate and subterranean moisture make termite attacks, water seepage, and wood rotting a frequent nightmare. Our signature UPVC & PVC interior solutions are 100% water and fire resistant, completely eliminating these hazards while providing modern, high-gloss or matte architectural finishes.

Materials & Safety

Kommerling UPVC Profiles, Century Club Prime Marine Ply, Greenply 710 BWP (Class-1 Fire Rated).

Ideal Applications

Kitchen Sink Units, Bathroom Vanities, Wet Balconies, Full Wardrobes.

2. Modular Kitchen Design

We engineer culinary spaces that harmonize daily cooking efficiency with stunning visual appeal. Layouts follow strict golden triangle ergonomics (hob, sink, refrigerator) with maximum corner storage utilization.

Hardware & Safety

Hettich InnoTech Drawers, Häfele Hinges, 100% Boiling Water & Fire Resistant Core.

Shutter Finishes

High Gloss Acrylic, Anti-Fingerprint Matte PU, Heat & Fire-Safe Quartz Surfaces.

Integrated Elements

Ceiling Materials & Safety

Lighting Fixtures

Dimmable Magnetic Track Lights, 3000K Warm Cove LEDs, Low-Heat Drivers.

Panelling Options

Partitions
